============================

Requested window: a8175ee2e4af..64952a192936 (inclusive)

The following commits supply the cumulative BPF integration. They are folded
into one target-adapted commit to avoid replaying the existing 4.9 backports
and intermediate add/revert pairs. Original copyright and license notices
remain in the sources.

Excluded: unrelated GPU, wireless driver, display, EROFS, Incremental FS, and
cgroup-v2 modernization; broad vmalloc allocator replacement; redundant early
BPF_OBJ_GET_INFO and Kprobes/endian add/revert pairs.

The kvmalloc/set_memory APIs are separately adapted from 0a6fe978d575 and
36f2ecd2caa3 without the allocator rewrite. The Android uname shim is separately
picked from 7e08c463294f, 3d8396c100fa, 6da514b59391, e5e06bfdab5e, 64952a192936.
